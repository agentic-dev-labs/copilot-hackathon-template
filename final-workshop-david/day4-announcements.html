WHAT STAYS, WHAT CHANGES

The stages stay. The jobs change.

Planning does not disappear. It becomes goals and acceptance criteria, and people spend their attention on judgment.

🎯

Plan

Person: choose the right problems, settle trade-offs.
Output: goals and acceptance criteria.

🔧

Build

Agents: implement. Person: guides key design decisions.
Output: a pull request, with context.

✅

Verify

Agents: test and gather evidence. Person: “is it what we intended?”
Output: auditable evidence.

📡

Observe

Person: interpret signals, decide what ships.
Output: outcomes that shape the next goals.

The glue:a shared knowledge layer that every stage reads from and adds to. “Autonomy does not remove that structure.”
Tim Bozarth, CVP CoreAI & EngThrive, Microsoft · Command Line, 27 Oct 2026
