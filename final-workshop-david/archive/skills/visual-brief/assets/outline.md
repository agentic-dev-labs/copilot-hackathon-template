# Five-slide Outline

This is a structure to adapt, not source evidence or a completed learner solution.

1. **The question and headline facts:** name the company/topic, audience, and reporting window.
2. **Performance over time:** one comparable measure or paired charts, units and source table.
3. **What changed:** a short sourced timeline; distinguish observations from explanations.
4. **Priorities and investment:** what the chosen sources actually support; keep gaps visible.
5. **Latest position and questions:** separately date the latest official update and management
   guidance; list what the documents cannot settle. With no current source, say so.

For four slides combine the timeline and priorities. Avoid a title-only slide that consumes
one fifth of the output. Each slide answers one question with a visual and a short explanation.
References stay close to the claims; charts also have a readable table for inspection.
