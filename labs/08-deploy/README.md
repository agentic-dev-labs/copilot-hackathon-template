# Lab 08: Save, Share, and Keep Building

**Objective:** Save your work on GitHub, show one feature you checked, and keep improving it.

**Time-box:** 45 minutes

**Prerequisites:** Your page and checks from Labs 00-02, in the same Codespace.

This lab runs on **Day 1**, right after Lab 02; the lab numbers are just names. At 14:45 everyone
moves to pair sharing. The presenter shows each step, then pauses for your turn.

## Steps

### 1. Save Your Work on GitHub (15 minutes)

1. Save your files. Open **Source Control** and read what actually changed, not only what Chat
   said. You should see only your website files and your note, with no real or private information.
2. Select **+** next to each file you checked, type a short message, select **Commit**, then
   **... > Push**.
3. Open your repository on GitHub. Your latest message is shown next to your files.
   If a push is rejected or mentions a conflict, ask a helper. Never force a push.

### 2. Keep Building (20 minutes)

4. Make one more small improvement and check it again in the preview. Then save, commit, and push.

### 3. Show It (10 minutes)

5. Show one feature to a neighbor. Explain what you expected, what you saw, and anything that
   does not work yet. Write your next idea in your brief instead of starting something big.

**Done when:** your work is on GitHub, your page still works in the preview, and you can explain
one real check. A deployment or saved browser state is not required.

## What you just learned

Pushing keeps your work safe on GitHub. Publishing, which is optional, puts a page on the
internet. Neither one proves the page works: you check that yourself.

## Optional extras

### Publish With a Helper

If your page is ready and a helper is free, you can publish it so others can open it with a
link. This is optional, and you can also do it on Tuesday or Thursday.

1. Ask a helper to check that your repository is ready to publish.
2. On GitHub, open **Actions > Deploy my app > Run workflow**. Choose **target: website**, leave
   **connect_dataverse** unchecked, and select **Run workflow** once.
3. When it finishes, open the run and select the link to your page.
4. Try your main feature again on the published page. It starts with the example entries,
   because your preview entries stay in your preview.
5. Agree with the helper how long the page stays online.

When you finish for the day, stop your Codespace at [github.com/codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces)
(**...** > **Stop codespace**). Closing the browser tab does not stop it, and stopping it does
not take down a published page.
